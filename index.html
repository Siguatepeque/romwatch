romwatch

A camera-based hand hypermobility screener

romwatch watches your hand through your webcam and checks it against two items of the Beighton score, the standard clinical hypermobility screening test: bending your thumb to your forearm, and bending your little finger back.

This is a screening aid, not a diagnosis. It is not a medical device and does not replace a clinician. All video processing happens locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
